postclassic_maya_ceramic_database_pottery.xlsx data comes from the now defunct Postclassic Ceramic Database that was ran by Dr. Leslie G. Cecil at Steven F. Austin State University.
I manually entered all the chemical data into the excel file from each ceramics pdf file (detailing the chemicals and specimen details).

All other datasets were downloaded from tdar (The Digital Antiquity Repository) or from MURR (University of Missouri Research Reactor).
